# ExpatsList

[ExpatsList](https://expatslist.org) is a global directory and community platform for expats, newcomers, digital nomads, and remote workers living abroad. It connects people relocating to or already living in a country with local, expat-friendly businesses and services, and with each other through community questions, recommendations, and classifieds. Coverage spans Cambodia, Canada, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Indonesia, Laos, Mexico, Panama, Poland, Puerto Rico, Spain, Thailand, the USA, and Vietnam, with dedicated pages for major expat destination cities in each country.

## Finding a business or service

Visitors browse by country, for example [expatslist.org/mexico](https://expatslist.org/mexico), or by city, then narrow down by category such as immigration lawyers, real estate agents, doctors, dentists, international schools, movers, accountants, or coworking spaces. Site search at [/search](https://expatslist.org/search) finds listings directly by name, service, or location, so a user does not have to know which city page a business is on. Each listing shows the business's services, location, and how to get in touch.

## Listing a business

An owner adds their business through the "List Your Business" flow at [/list-business](https://expatslist.org/list-business) or [/add-your-business](https://expatslist.org/add-your-business). If a business is already listed but not yet managed by its owner, the owner can claim it at [/claim](https://expatslist.org/claim) to take control, update details, and respond as the business.

## Getting verified

Businesses can apply for a verified badge, which signals to visitors that a listing has been confirmed as real, active, and expat-friendly. It is a quick trust signal when comparing several listings in the same category.

## Community questions

The Questions section at [/questions](https://expatslist.org/questions) lets people ask real questions about living, working, or relocating to a specific place (visa rules, neighborhoods, cost of living, schools, healthcare) and get answers from residents, other newcomers, and locals. It works like a community Q&A board scoped to expat life in each city or country.

## Recommendations

[/recommendations](https://expatslist.org/recommendations) surfaces businesses and services other users vouch for, useful when someone wants a trusted starting point rather than browsing an entire category cold.

## Classifieds

Classifieds at [/classifieds](https://expatslist.org/classifieds) cover buy and sell, housing, jobs, and community postings aimed at expats. Legitimate classifieds always include a direct way to make contact, such as a phone number, email, WhatsApp number, or external link; a listing with no direct contact method is not something a user can act on.

## Staying safe

The Safety section at [/safety](https://expatslist.org/safety) has guidance for vetting a listing or classified before meeting someone or sending money.

## Accounts

Users manage their profile, preferences, listings, and classifieds from [/account](https://expatslist.org/account) and [/profile](https://expatslist.org/profile).
